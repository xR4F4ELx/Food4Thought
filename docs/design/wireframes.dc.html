Food4Thought — implementation spec

One reference per screen: what it does, its states, and the data it binds to. Numbers shown in the wireframes are illustrative; the source of truth is the calc + schema noted below. Nothing here overrides the brief's non-goals (no BMI shown, no finish dates, no confetti).

Global model — the balance (debt / credit)

ConceptRule
Signed balanceOne integer, kcal. Negative = debt owed, zero = square, positive = credit. Rendered as the "Balance" ring on Home and the balance hero on Activity.
Accrues fromDaily overage only: consumed − dailyTarget when positive rolls into debt at day close (or live on Home).
Cleared / built byExercise burn from Apple Health active energy. Deducts debt first; any remainder becomes credit.
LifetimeRolls indefinitely (chosen: 8b). Never auto-resets. Display caps the "focus to clear now" at ~2 days of overage so it never reads hopeless.
Credit cap+500 kcal (~1 day). Burn past the cap is dropped — it's already reflected in weight trend. Show cap meter on Activity.
NeverExercise does not add to today's food target. dailyTarget is fixed; activity level is already priced into it via TDEE.
Sign & color: debt uses --over/--debt (red/graphite), credit uses --fat (green). The ring is a single component driven by a signed value with a three-way color/label switch at 0.

Targets & calculation

ValueSource
Daily kcalTDEECalculator (BMR × ActivityLevel × goal adjust). Persisted per goal_sets row.
Macros P/C/FMacroTargets derived from kcal + goal. Colors: protein --pro, carbs --carb, fat --fat.
Meal slotsMealSchedule / MealSchedulePreset + TimeOfDay. Home renders one row per slot; supports 1 (OMAD) to N. expectedShare drives "on pace".
RecalibrationCompares projected vs observed kg/wk from body_metrics weight history. Manual, tap-to-accept. Writes a new goal_sets row; old rows stay queryable.

7a / 7b / 7cEmpty & reminder7a: first-run Home, rings at rest, one quiet prompt. 7b: Trends "not enough data" with progress + one action. 7c: slot-aware reminder, respects 60-min grace, skips if already logged.

Copy & tone guardrails

Plain, factual, no shame. Burn figures always labelled estimate. No streak pressure, no badges, no confetti. Over-days are stated, not scolded. Medical disclaimer on any target screen. Reminders fire at most once per slot and never nag.
